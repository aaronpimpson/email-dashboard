Write triage rules for your emails here in plain English, then save this file as rules.txt.
The AI applies them on every scan, and they override its default judgment. Examples:

ALWAYS flag as needs reply:
- Any email from DocuSign or Adobe Sign: signature requests, reminders, and final signed copies. Start the reason with "URGENT:".
- Anything from my accountant (jane@example.com), even if it looks automated.

NEVER flag:
- Verification codes and login alerts.
- The Monday sales newsletter, even though a human sends it.

Rules can be account-specific:
- The DocuSign rule does NOT apply to my personal account (me@gmail.com); treat those as normal automated mail.
